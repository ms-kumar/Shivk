abstract: >-
  ClothFormer tackles human outfit parsing with a boundary-aware self-attention
  network that preserves garment edges while modeling long-range dependencies
  across body regions and clothing items. The boundary-aware design improves
  segmentation around fine garment boundaries where standard parsers bleed
  across adjacent regions. The model uses a self-attention mechanism that
  attends to clothing regions while suppressing irrelevant background context,
  achieving improved parsing accuracy on standard benchmarks.
