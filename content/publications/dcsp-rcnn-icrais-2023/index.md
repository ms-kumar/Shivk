abstract: >-
  DCSP-RCNN extends person detection by jointly parsing color, size, and
  pattern characteristics alongside bounding boxes. Modeling these attributes
  explicitly helps disambiguate people in crowded scenes and supports
  attribute-aware downstream tasks such as retrieval and tracking. The network
  predicts person masks and per-pixel color, size, and pattern descriptors
  simultaneously, enabling robust detection when color or scale varies.
